A flower people can associate with the name.

Five soft blue petals and a golden center. Its silhouette holds up without lettering, so the favicon and app icon belong to the same identity. Fraunces brings a warm, slightly unusual rhythm to the wordmark.

Character in the brand. Clarity in the editor.

Use the display font for the name and occasional headings. Keep Onest for menus and controls, and keep the reader’s font preference for notes.
