"You" means the legal entity that signs this agreement, together with every entity
that controls it, is controlled by it, or is under common control with it.
"Control" means owning more than half of an entity's outstanding shares or other
voting interests, or having the power to direct its management, by contract or
otherwise.

"Designated Contributors" means the people listed in Schedule A, as You update it
under section 8.

"Contribution" means any work of authorship, including a change or addition to an
existing work, that a Designated Contributor intentionally submits to Wrightworks
for inclusion in the Project on Your behalf. Submitting includes pull requests,
patches, and any other written, electronic or verbal communication sent to the
Project, its repositories, issue trackers or maintainers in order to discuss or
improve the Project. Anything clearly marked in writing as "Not a Contribution"
when it is submitted is not a Contribution.

## 8. Keeping this agreement current

You will tell Wrightworks, through the point of contact below, when a person
should be added to or removed from Schedule A, and if You learn of any fact or
circumstance that would make Your representations in this agreement inaccurate in
any respect. Removing a person ends coverage for their later submissions only; the
licences for Contributions already submitted continue.

## How to sign

Fill in and sign the form and Schedule A, then ask the maintainers how to send it
by opening a GitHub issue titled "Corporate CLA". Put no personal or organization
details in the issue; a maintainer arranges a private way to receive the form.
Once Wrightworks records the agreement, the CLA check on pull requests accepts the
GitHub accounts in Schedule A.
